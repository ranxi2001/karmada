The size of the leaked taint fixture is small but sufficient to change this boundary-sensitive test. It is a [3-replica Deployment requesting `10m` CPU per replica](https://github.com/karmada-io/karmada/blob/3d4d14d746de507164abf40c1017b1f2b0e47e3a/test/helper/resource.go#L190-L218). Once the old binding was rescheduled to all three clusters, the scheduler recorded `30m` per cluster, or 9 assumed replicas / `90m` in total.

The affected Flink test [targets only member1, adds `200m` per FlinkDeployment, and stops at the first no-fit](https://github.com/karmada-io/karmada/blob/3d4d14d746de507164abf40c1017b1f2b0e47e3a/test/e2e/suites/base/estimator_test.go#L379-L515). The relevant foreign reservation is therefore member1's `30m`, not the three-cluster `90m`. It does not need to exhaust member1 by itself; it only needs to push one `200m` attempt over the capacity boundary. In the [official job](https://github.com/karmada-io/karmada/actions/runs/28998390044/job/86054168911), that `30m` assumption was present at the Flink no-fit at `07:15:22.998`. After it disappeared, the `200m` probe fit at `07:15:24.668`, showing that the Flink loop had stopped at a boundary its own workloads had not reached alone.
